# 📚 ESL Lesson Plan: Real-Life Vocabulary, Collocations & Idioms

**Target Level:** CEFR B1 - B2 (Intermediate / Upper-Intermediate)  
**Target Audience:** Adult ESL / EFL Learners (specifically with Brazilian Portuguese L1 context)  
**Lesson Duration:** 60 Minutes (Flexible to 90 min)  
**Interactive App:** Open [`index.html`](file:///e:/Documents/mariana/index.html) in any web browser.

---

## 🎯 Lesson Objectives
By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1. **Identify and correctly use 16 high-frequency collocations & idioms** across three key thematic areas: *Family & Relationships*, *Work & Career*, and *Daily Routines*.
2. **Pronounce and naturally stress compound nouns and phrasal verbs** (e.g., *breadwinner*, *take after*, *unwind*).
3. **Distinguish subtle meaning differences** and avoid false cognates when translating to Portuguese.
4. **Engage in 15+ minutes of spontaneous speaking/discussion** using sentence frames and target idioms.

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step 60-Minute Lesson Procedure (PPP Framework)

### Phase 1: Warm-Up & Schema Activation (8 mins)
- **Activity:** *Quick-Fire Questions*
- **Instructions:** Ask students:
  1. *"Who is the earliest riser in your house?"*
  2. *"What is the most tiring chore you have to do outside the house?"*
  3. *"How long does it take you to travel to school or work every day?"*
- **Goal:** Activate existing schema around daily routines and family life without introducing formal jargon yet.

---

### Phase 2: Presentation & Interactive Discovery (15 mins)
- **App Tool:** Launch [`index.html`](file:///e:/Documents/mariana/index.html) -> **🎴 Flashcards Tab**
- **Teacher Action:**
  1. Click through the flashcard modules (*Family*, *Work*, *Daily*).
  2. Click the **🔊 Audio button** on each card to model authentic pronunciation and intonation.
  3. Ask students to repeat chorally, then individually.
  4. Prompt students to guess the Portuguese equivalent before clicking **👁️ Reveal Portuguese (🇧🇷)**.
  5. Highlight collocations: *Heavy workload* (not *big workload*), *Meet a deadline* (not *reach a deadline*), *Run errands* (not *make errands*).

---

### Phase 3: Controlled Practice & Dialogue Analysis (15 mins)
- **App Tool:** **📝 Practice Quiz Tab** & **💬 Dialogues Tab**
- **Activity 3A:** Students complete the 5 interactive multiple-choice quiz questions. Discuss why wrong distractors do not fit.
- **Activity 3B:** Pair work reading of the dialogue between *Mariana* and *Lucas*:
  - Student A reads Mariana; Student B reads Lucas.
  - Swap roles and try to replace 2 words with their own real-life situation.

---

### Phase 4: Free Production & Conversational Fluency (17 mins)
- **App Tool:** **🗣️ Speaking Tasks Tab**
- **Pair Discussion Prompts:**
  1. **Genetics & Personality:** *"Who in your family do you take after the most? Are you the spitting image of anyone in your extended family?"*
  2. **Workplace Pressure:** *"When you have a heavy workload and a tight deadline, do you prefer working overtime or delegating tasks?"*
  3. **Lifestyle & Self-care:** *"Are you an early bird or a night owl? How do you unwind after running errands all Saturday morning?"*
- **Teacher Role:** Monitor pairs, take notes on good language usage and pronunciation slips for delayed correction.

---

### Phase 5: Feedback & Wrap-up (5 mins)
- **Delayed Error Correction:** Write 3 anonymous student errors on the board and elicit corrections as a group.
- **Exit Ticket:** Each student must produce 1 sentence using at least one target idiom describing their plan for this upcoming weekend.

### Exercise A: Fill in the Blanks
*Fill in the blank with the best word from the box:*  
`[ breadwinner | take after | overtime | commute | unwind | run errands | deadline | spitting image ]`

1. My daily ____________ takes 45 minutes on the subway every morning.
2. Gabriel looks just like his father; he is the ____________ of him!
3. I worked 8 hours of ____________ this week to finish the financial report.
4. On Saturday mornings, I usually ____________ like picking up groceries and mailing packages.
5. In order to ____________ after stressful meetings, I enjoy doing yoga.
6. She really ____________ her mother when it comes to patience and kindness.
7. We must submit the project before the 5:00 PM ____________.
8. When my father was sick, my mother became the sole ____________ of our family.

#### ✅ Answer Key:
1. *commute*
2. *spitting image*
3. *overtime*
4. *run errands*
5. *unwind*
6. *takes after*
7. *deadline*
8. *breadwinner*
